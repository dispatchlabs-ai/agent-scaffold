# Company writing style

Lead with what people can do. Prefer short sentences, concrete verbs and familiar
words. Keep product claims tied to the facts supplied in the conversation.

- Say “Export a report as CSV” rather than “Unlock powerful reporting.”
- Explain any action a reader must take.
- Keep limitations visible. Never invent availability, dates or performance.
- Preserve the names and version numbers the user supplied.

This shared example applies to every agent that enables skills. Replace it with
your company's actual writing guidance.
